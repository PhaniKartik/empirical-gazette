# The Empirical Gazette — Business Roadmap

## Principle

Keep the newspaper free while the audience is small. Build the audience asset first; monetize only after there is measurable reader demand.

## Stage 1 — 0 to ~1,000 engaged readers

- Free daily editions.
- Email capture as the primary growth asset.
- Search-friendly archive and RSS.
- Privacy-friendly analytics.
- Strong share links and recognizable daily dispatch numbering.
- No intrusive ads and no paywall.

## Stage 2 — ~1,000 to 5,000 engaged readers

Test one light revenue stream:

1. reader support / founding members, or
2. one tasteful sponsor per edition.

Keep the core newspaper free.

## Stage 3 — ~5,000 to 20,000 engaged readers

Add a premium layer rather than locking the whole newspaper:

- weekly long-form historical investigation;
- annotated primary-source dossier;
- printable collector's edition PDF;
- monthly historical timeline / research pack;
- members-only archive tools.

## Stage 4 — 20,000+ engaged readers

Potential business lines:

- premium subscriptions;
- sponsorships from museums, publishers, universities, scientific organizations, and education companies;
- institutional/group subscriptions;
- paid classroom/educator packs;
- museum or publisher partnerships;
- print-on-demand annual volumes;
- licensing of selected historical research packages.

## Editorial rule

Never let a sponsor, affiliate relationship, or premium tier influence historical claims, source selection, or fact-checking. Trust is the product.

## Technical rule

Keep the audience relationship portable. The site has provider-neutral configuration for email, analytics, support, premium, and sponsors so a future migration from one service to another does not require redesigning the newspaper.
